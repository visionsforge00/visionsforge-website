❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with VisionsForge — on ownership, data, pricing, and how projects actually run.

Do I own the code and AI system after the project is delivered?

Yes. Once a project is delivered and paid for, the code and the system belong entirely to you — there's no ongoing licensing fee or vendor lock-in tied to VisionsForge.

Is my customer data safe with a custom-built AI system?

Yes. Since the system is built specifically for your business, data storage and access can be scoped exactly to your requirements, instead of relying on a third-party platform's default policies.

Do you charge a fixed price or hourly for automation projects?

Projects are typically quoted as a fixed price based on scope, agreed on upfront — so there are no surprise hourly bills partway through.

Can an AI agent handle both Hindi and English customer messages?

Yes. AI agents can be built to understand and reply in Hindi, English, or Hinglish — matched to how your customers actually communicate.

Can you integrate automation with tools I already use, like Tally, Google Sheets, or Zoho?

In most cases, yes. AI agents and automation workflows can be connected to the tools your business already runs on, instead of forcing you to switch platforms.

Do you work with clients outside India remotely?

Yes. VisionsForge works with clients remotely worldwide — all communication, planning, and delivery happen online.
