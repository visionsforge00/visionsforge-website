Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

VisionsForge ("we", "us", "our") operates visionsforge.in. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The subject and message you submit

This information is sent securely to a private Google Sheet that only VisionsForge has access to. It is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Appointment Booking

If you book an appointment through our "Book an Appointment" button, you're redirected to Google Calendar's booking page. Any information you provide there (name, email, and any details you add) is collected and processed by Google, governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and shared with VisionsForge to schedule and manage the appointment.

Third-Party Services

This website uses a small number of third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography. Loading fonts may involve your browser connecting to Google's servers.
  • Google Calendar — for appointment booking, as described above.
  • Google Apps Script / Google Sheets — to receive and store contact form submissions.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website does not currently use cookies for tracking or advertising. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained in our private Google Sheet for as long as needed to respond to and manage inquiries. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've submitted to us by emailing contact@visionsforge.in.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out at contact@visionsforge.in or via WhatsApp.
